Cocoa Brownies

One 9" x 13" pan · Bake 28–30 min

You Will Need

Ingredients

Granulated sugar, 2 1/4 cups (447g)
Dutch-process cocoa, 1 1/4 cups (106g)
Baking powder, 1 teaspoon
Salt, 1/2 tsp (omit if using salted butter)
All-purpose flour, 1 1/2 cups (180g)
Chopped walnuts or pecans and/or chocolate chips, 2 cups total (optional)
Large eggs, 5
Water, 1/2 cup (113g)
Salted butter, 2 sticks
Vanilla extract, 1 tablespoon (14g)

Equipment

9" x 13" pan
Electric mixer or whisk
Cooling rack

Cook

Preheat oven

1.Preheat to 350°F (177°C).

Make the Batter

2.Grease the pan.
In a Large Bowl
3.Granulated sugar
In a Small Bowl
4.Dutch-process cocoa
5.Baking powder
6.Salt
In a Medium Bowl
7.All-purpose flour
8.Walnuts, pecans, and/or chocolate chips (2 cups total)
9.Melt the butter.
10.Combine the melted butter and sugar in the large bowl, stirring until smooth.
11.Stir in the small bowl's cocoa mixture and the vanilla extract.
12.Beat in the eggs one at a time.
13.Scrape the bowl, then stir in the water until smooth.
14.Toss the mix-ins in the medium bowl's flour to coat, then stir them into the batter until thoroughly combined.
15.Spoon the batter into the prepared pan.

Bake

16.Bake for 28 to 30 minutes, until the top is dry and the edges have started to pull away from the sides, or a toothpick inserted into the center comes out clean.

Cool

17.Remove the brownies from the oven and cool them on a rack before cutting and serving. Let the brownies cool fully before cutting — warm, they're closer to chocolate cake; as they settle while cooling they become the dense, fudgy cake-type brownie.
18.Glaze or frost, if desired.
