Chana Masala

Makes 2-4 servings · Serve over rice with a dollop of yogurt · Garnish with fresh cilantro

You Will Need

Ingredients

Onion (red or white), 1 medium (~1.5 cups)
Cilantro, 3 Tbsp (optional)
Cumin seed, 1 tsp
Turmeric, 1/2 tsp
Ginger powder, 1 tsp
Garlic powder, 1 tsp
Coriander powder, 1 tsp
Cumin powder, 1 tsp
Cayenne pepper, 1/4 tsp
Garam masala, 1 tsp
Olive oil or ghee, 2 Tbsp
Tomatoes, 14.5 oz can
Chickpeas, 15 oz can
Salt
Water
Rice, for serving
Yogurt, for serving

Prep

· Finely chop the onion.
· Into a small bowl, measure the cumin seed (1 tsp).
· Into a small bowl, measure the turmeric (1/2 tsp).
· Into a small bowl, measure the ginger powder (1 tsp) and garlic powder (1 tsp). Set aside together.
· Into a small bowl, measure the coriander powder (1 tsp).
· Into a small bowl, measure the cumin powder (1 tsp).
· Into a small bowl, measure the cayenne pepper (1/4 tsp).
· Into a small bowl, measure the garam masala (1 tsp).
· Open the tomato can. Open the chickpea can, reserving 1 Tbsp of the liquid, then drain and rinse the chickpeas.
· If using cilantro, roughly chop 3 Tbsp and set aside.

Cook

Prepare

1.Get your rice started according to package directions.

Build the Curry

2.Toast the cumin seeds in a dry skillet over medium heat until they start popping.
3.Add the olive oil or ghee and let it get hot.
4.Add the onion and turmeric. Cook for 3-4 minutes.
5.Add the ginger and garlic powder and stir. Add the coriander powder and stir to release the aromas. Then add the cumin powder and stir.
6.Add the tomatoes and cayenne. Cook for 6-8 minutes until the tomatoes are soft and the oil separates.
7.Add the chickpeas, reserved chickpea liquid, salt, and enough water to just cover the chickpeas.
8.Cover the pan and cook for 10-12 minutes until the chickpeas are soft.
9.Uncover and stir in the garam masala. Let the sauce thicken to your liking.

Serve

10.Serve over rice with yogurt.
11.If using fresh cilantro, add it now.
